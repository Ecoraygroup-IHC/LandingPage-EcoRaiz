Problem

Why do indoor plants die?

Many plants deteriorate because warning signs appear late, and general advice does not consider the real microclimate of each home, classroom or business.

⏱️

Lack of monitoring

Users do not always check their plants on time, and environmental changes can go unnoticed.

💦

Incorrect watering

Too much or too little water affects plant health and can waste resources.

🌤️

Limited light

Enclosed urban spaces do not always provide the right exposure for each species.

🔎

Generic information

Tutorials do not always explain what to do according to the user's real environment.

📅

Lack of time

Daily routines make constant and organized supervision difficult.

⚠️

Late deterioration

When leaves change color or wilt, it can already be difficult to recover the plant.

Solution

Frequently asked questions

Resolve your questions before starting

EcoRaíz is designed to turn technical information into clear decisions.

Do I need botany knowledge to use EcoRaíz?

No. EcoRaíz is designed for users without technical knowledge.

What variables does EcoRaíz monitor?

Humidity, light, temperature and general crop status.

Does EcoRaíz work for plant businesses?

Yes. It helps manage live inventory, detect risks and reduce losses.

Can I use EcoRaíz in classes or research?

Yes. It includes visual dashboards, historical charts and reports.

Does the app replace the user?

No. It helps make better decisions, but the user keeps final control.
